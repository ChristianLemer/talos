# Intention

Talos is the **graphical promoter and installer**: it installs, repairs, and
shows the state of a machine — *by showing* what it does. It knows no client;
everything specific enters by extension. Its first payload is Tekton.

It addresses the whole gaussian of a transition — and first of all those who do
not yet live inside the fully interactive textual interface. Where Chiron speaks
with those who inhabit the text, Talos holds out the ramp to those who have not
yet crossed.

Talos does, efficiently, the things that are **deterministic, common, and
predictable** — what a good many people ask for. The bespoke, the judgment-bound,
the deep are not its territory: they lie out at sea, and that is where the textual
interface takes over. The end of the deterministic is the boundary of Talos.

It is a refuge: a familiar interface to return to when one is lost. Its hull does
not change; its cargo grows. Talos evolves, and each person activates what they
are ready for — the tool adjusts to the phase reached, never forcing the open sea.

We begin with the minimum common to all: **the installer**.
